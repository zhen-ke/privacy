# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all applications published by zhen-ke
(hereinafter referred to as "the Apps"). It explains our policies
regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you
use our Apps.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or
usage information.**

- **No Personal Data:** The Apps do not require you to create an
  account, register, or provide any personal information (such as your
  name, email address, phone number, or location).
- **Local Processing:** Your content — including records, notes,
  settings, and on-device computations — remains strictly on your
  device and is never uploaded to external servers. We operate no
  servers that receive your data.
- **User-Initiated Feedback (where offered):** Some Apps may offer an
  optional "Send Feedback" feature. It generates an email draft
  containing diagnostic details (device model, OS version, app
  version) for your preview. Information is transmitted only when you
  choose to send the email from your own email client.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Tracking

- The Apps do not integrate third-party analytics, tracking tools, or
  advertising networks.
- We do not sell, trade, or rent any user information to third parties.
- Where noted below, an App may contact Apple's own services (App
  Store / iTunes APIs) for product information. Those requests go to
  Apple, not to us — no user data ever reaches us.

## 3. Permissions

The Apps only request device permissions necessary for their core
functionality. Permissions are never used to harvest or track personal
information.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Because the Apps do not collect personal data, they comply with the
Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and are safe for
users of all ages, including children under 13.
